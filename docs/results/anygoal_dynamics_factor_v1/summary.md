## Dynamics decision

**NO-GO.** The fixed Extract-Peg goal is bank-solvable under the welded 25-substep dynamics. Under the training integrator, bank solvability is 0.100, below the 0.80 floor, so opportunity regret there is not a ranking result. The demonstrated decision failure sits on a transition the Insert-Peg training data do not support.

This decision concerns the fixed Extract-Peg goal only. A low unconditional regret under the training integrator is not a ranking success when solvability is below 0.80. Whether the same model ranks goals generated by that integrator is a separate insert-anchor measurement and is not in this table.
